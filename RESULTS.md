## Energy profiles

- NUCLEO-L4R5ZI: 3.0 V × 17.528 mA (STM32CubeMX MCU RUN plus enabled peripherals).
- STM32F746G-DISCO: 3.3 V × 110.538 mA (STM32CubeMX MCU RUN plus enabled peripherals).
- STM32H573I-DK: 3.0 V × 29.2 mA (datasheet typical LDO current for the MCU; USART current is not quantified).

Energy in µJ is `voltage (V) × current (mA) × mean DWT time (µs) / 1000`. The voltage/current profiles differ, so cross-board numbers do not isolate processor or clock effects. The original per-experiment energy reports are retained in Italian as source records.

The archived per-inference CSV files and summaries provide the underlying observations for independent recalculation of accuracy, mean DWT time, and energy.
